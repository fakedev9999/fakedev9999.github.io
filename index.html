Taehoon Kim

I'm a software engineer at Succinct, working on ZK proving systems and rollup infrastructure. I led the development of OP Succinct Lite, a ZK fault proof system for OP Stack rollups, and contribute to OP Succinct.

Previously, I was a ZK engineer at Kroma, where I worked on Tachyon and zkVM-based proving systems. I have an M.S. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from Seoul National University and a B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from POSTECH.
